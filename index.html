The Kitchen

Cooked over embers, served in shadow.

Phantom's Kitchen opened in a room that used to be a coal cellar, and it still cooks like one. Every dish crosses an open flame before it crosses the pass, and the dining room stays lit by candle and coal-glow alone.

There is no printed history on the wall and no house legend to recite. Only a short, changing menu, a long charcoal grill, and a kitchen that prefers to let the food explain itself.

"We don't season for memory. We season so you can't quite recall what a tomato tasted like before this one."Head Chef, Phantom's Kitchen
